# Controllers Http Requests

## Recovery readiness and escalation authority
The responsible Nurse owns recovery readiness and, in Checkpoint 5.5, uncomplicated discharge; routine Doctor approval is forbidden. A Nurse may mark an in-progress recovery ready only when criteria are met and no open escalation exists. Clinical concerns create a durable open escalation; an authorized Doctor may resolve it only back to Nursing monitoring/readiness, never discharge the patient. Readiness is not discharge.
